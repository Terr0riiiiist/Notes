### Idioms

**to hit it off**: to get along well with someone from the start; to become friends
- *They hit it off immediately.*

**through thick and thin**: through many difficult times over a long period
- *She stood by me through thick and thin.*

**close-knit** *(adj.)*: bound together by intimate social or cultural ties
- *a close-knit family that constantly keeps in touch*

**to have someone's back** *(mainly US, informal)*: to be ready to protect or defend someone
- *Don't worry. I've got your back.*

**like two peas in a pod**: used to say that two people or things are very similar to each other
- *My brother and I are two peas in a pod. We both like the same things.*

**to clear the air**: to remove elements of hostility, tension, confusion, or uncertainty
- *We had a long meeting to clear the air.*

**friends in high places** *(noun phrase)*: people a person knows who have social or political influence or power
- *She got the job because she has friends in high places.*

**to see eye to eye**: to have a common viewpoint; to agree
- *We don't always see eye to eye on politics.*
