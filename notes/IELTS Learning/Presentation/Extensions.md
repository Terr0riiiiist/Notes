
**Hello, everyone**. You can call me Maxwell Laurence. "Maxwell" is in honor of the great physician Clerk Maxwell ,and I just want my English name sounds more British, so 
Laurence is a good choice.

I'm Han Chinese.
My hometown is Hubei, Huanggang, very close to Wuhan. So it's doesn't need much effort to trip home at weekends, which only takes 1 hour and half to knock my home's gate. Walking on the street you don't worry about getting hit by e-bikes, unlike Wuhan. In fact, there aren't many people walking even in the daytime.

In my personal point of view, there isn't many  attractions that worth you specifically pay a visit to. For example , Dongpo Chibi is a total tourist trap. What I prefer is strolling along the riverside. Along the Yangtze River, there is a vast forest park. Beyond the dense woods, the full river view comes. In the blue hour, walking along the riverbank, the dim light from river islands and massive steel arms at the factories at the opposite bank are faintly visible. It's so atmospheric, right? In summer, the sky is often filled with beautiful, fiery sunset clouds. You may also see fireflies in the woods. As you stroll along the riverbank, you may occasionally come across abandoned houses that have stood there for decades. To be honest, the river means a lot to me. It became a necessary part of my soul long time ago, and I feel much closer to the true self. The vast stretch of forest and wide river surface will accept all my emotions.

As for visitors, I recommend you enjoying the slow pace of live, extremely low cost of living and the  life untouched by urbanized. Even in the so called downtown, there's still few skyscrapers.

The only hope is that the city stays as same as in my memory.
