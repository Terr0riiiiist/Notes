#### Material 1.
**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">C</span>ompost**: [noun] a mixture of decayed (= destroyed by natural processes) plants, food, etc. that can be added to soil to help plants grow 堆肥

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">L</span>oads of**: [quantifier] a <font color="#f79646">large amount </font>or a great number of something
        *We have loads of time before the train leaves.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">R</span>ecipe**: [noun] **recipe for something**   a <font color="#f79646">method</font> or an <font color="#f79646">idea</font> that seems likely to have a <font color="#de7802">particular result</font>
        *Driving too fast is a recipe for disaster.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">C</span>rust**: [noun] the hard outer surface of bread 面包皮
        *We saved a few crusts of bread for the birds.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">S</span>prinkle**: [transitive] to shake small pieces of something or drops of a liquid on something
        **sprinkle A on/onto/over B** *Sprinkle chocolate on top of the cake.*
        **sprinkle B with A** *She sprinkled the strawberries with sugar.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">G</span>rind**: [grind-ground-ground] **grind something (down/up) | grind something (to/into something)** to <font color="#f79646">break or press something into very small pieces</font> between two hard surfaces or using a special machine
        *She grinds fresh coffee beans every morning.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">I</span>n the lines of**: [idiom] similar to,roughly like
        *His latest film is in the lines of his earlier comedies.*

#### Material 2.

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">E</span>nroll**: [intransitive, transitive] to arrange for yourself or for somebody else to officially <font color="#f79646">join</font> a course, school, etc.
        *She enrolled in an English course last month.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">P</span>rovost**: [noun] a senior official who manages the affairs of some colleges and universities
        *The provost oversees teaching and research at the college.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">E</span>ntice**: [verb] to persuade somebody/something to go somewhere or to do something, usually by offering them something SYN.<font color="#6425d0">persuade</font>
        *The store enticed customers with huge discounts.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">M</span>old**: [countable] a container that you pour a liquid or soft substance into, which then becomes solid in the <font color="#de7802">same shape as the container</font>, for example when it is cooled or cooked
        *A clay mold is used for casting bronze statues.*

#### Material 3.

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">B</span>last**: [countable] an <font color="#de7802">explosion</font> or a powerful movement of air caused by an explosion
        **blast off** a rocket or spacecraft leaves the ground and starts its flight into space
        *The rocket blasted off successfully.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">D</span>ock**: [countable] a part of a port where ships are repaired, or where goods are put onto or taken off them

#### Material 4.

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">R</span>ib**: [countable] any of the <font color="#de7802">curved bones</font> that are connected to the [spine](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/spine "spine definition") and surround the chest. 肋骨

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">F</span>ryer**: [noun] a large deep pan used for frying food in. 油炸锅

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">D</span>iesel**: [uncountable] a type of heavy oil used as a fuel instead of petrol. 柴油
        **biodiesel** 生物柴油

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">E</span>ncroach**: [intransitive] **encroach (on/upon something)** to slowly begin to cover more and more of an area
        *The growing town soon encroached on the surrounding countryside.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">C</span>anola**: [noun] plant with bright yellow flowers that is grown widely in North America and is used to make cooking oil, also called canola. 菜籽油

#### Material 5.

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">I</span>mpediment**: [noun] a problem, for example a [stammer](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/stammer_1 "stammer definition"), that makes it more difficult for somebody to speak, hear, etc.
        *The accident left him with a speech impediment.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">D</span>efect**: [noun] a <font color="#de7802">fault</font> in something or in the way it has been made that means that it is <font color="#de7802">not perfect</font>
        *The toy was recalled because of a safety defect.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">I</span>nterfere**: [verb] to get involved in and try to <font color="#de7802">influence a situation</font> that should not really involve you, in a way that annoys other people
        *Don't interfere in other people's business.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">P</span>rolong**: [verb] **prolong something** to make something last longer SYN.<font color="#6425d0">extend</font>
        *They hoped the treatment would prolong her life.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">P</span>rompt**: [transitive] to <font color="#de7802">make somebody decide</font> to do something; to cause something to happen
    **prompt somebody to do something** *Her father's struggles with mental health prompted her to pursue psychology as a career.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">D</span>ysfunctional**: [adjective] not working well or normally
        *He grew up in a dysfunctional family.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">S</span>ufferer**: [noun] a person who suffers, especially somebody who has a medical condition

#### Material 6.

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">D</span>epression**: [countable] (formal) a part of a <font color="#de7802">surface that is lower</font> than the parts around it
        *Rainwater collects in shallow depressions on the ground.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">P</span>redecessor**: [noun] a person who did a job before somebody else
        *The new principal is stricter than her predecessor.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">A</span>coustic**: [adjective] <font color="#de7802"> related to sound</font> or to the sense of hearing
        *Whales communicate using acoustic signals.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">S</span>huttle**: [noun]
        *1*. a [spacecraft](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/spacecraft "spacecraft definition") designed to make repeated journeys, for example between the earth and a space station
        *2*. a plane, bus or train that travels regularly between two places *I’m flying to Boston on the shuttle.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">T</span>ribute**: [uncountable, countable] **tribute (to somebody)** <font color="#de7802">an act, a statement</font> or <font color="#de7802">a gift</font> that is intended to show your love or respect, especially for a dead person
        *At her funeral her oldest friend paid tribute to her life and work.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">S</span>erenade**: [noun] a gentle <font color="#de7802">piece of music</font> in several parts, usually for a small group of instruments 小夜曲
