Catastrophic flash floods, devastated communities along the [Nepal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nepal)-China border, rescue crews are searching for people still missing, the flooding destroyed roads, knocked out power, and entire communities were cut off from help. Our Hanako Montgomery has more on how these floods weren't even caused by rain, but rather a massive **Himalayan** glacier that collapsed.

**Himalayan** [ˈhɪmǝleən] adj. 喜玛拉雅山脉的

Dozens of people begin to run. What comes next in this surveillance video is hard to imagine. A mountain of mud and water, taller than the building, crashes down, wiping out everything in its path, like something from a disaster film.

But this is a border crossing between China and Nepal. It's the result of a deadly flash flood that swept through Himalayan villages on the Nepal-China border on Wednesday. It's enormous force destroying homes and buildings, including in Nepal's [Rasuwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rasuwa_District) district.

Aerial footage captured from a helicopter over the district shows the immense **barrage** of water flooding the valley below. It's a popular area for hiking holidays. Hundreds of tourists, including foreign nationals, have been reported missing, according to an initial assessment by Nepal's tourism board.

**barrage** [bǝˈrɑʒ] n. 齐射式攻击；猛烈的攻击

**Nepalese** army helicopters are helping rescue citizens from rooftops in the flood-affected areas. The Red Cross is also deploying an emergency team with relief supplies. And in Tibet's [Gyirong port](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyirong_Port), China's President Xi Jinping has ordered **all-out** search and rescue efforts to find those who are missing after a mudslide caused major casualties, China's state news agency [Xinhua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinhua_News_Agency) reported.

**Nepalese** [ˌnɛpǝˈliz] adj. 尼泊尔（人）的，尼泊尔（语）的

**all-out** [ˈɔlˈaʊt] adj. 竭尽全力的；全部的；毫无保留的

The mudslide has cut off the region's roads, communications, and power, Xinhua said. Nepal's foreign minister said the deadly flash flood was caused by an earthquake, triggering a large landslide which blocked the [Bhotekoshi river](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhotekoshi_River). The full scale of the devastation is yet to become clear.
