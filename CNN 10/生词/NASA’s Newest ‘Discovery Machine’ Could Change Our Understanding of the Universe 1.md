# 
**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">F</span>aint**: that cannot be clearly seen, heard or smelt
    - **a faint glow/glimmer/light**
    - *a faint smell of perfume*
    - *We saw the faint outline of the mountain through the mist.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">O</span>therwise**: used to state what the result would be if something did not happen or if the situation were different     [adv.]
     **otherwise + adjective** → "would be [adjective] in a different situation"
     - *The technology makes otherwise impossible tasks possible.*
- = tasks that would be impossible without the technology
     - *The telescope detects otherwise invisible galaxies.*
- = galaxies that cannot be seen without this telescope

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">P</span>anorama**: a view of a wide area of land  n. 全景
     - *There is a superb panorama of the mountains from the hotel.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">E</span>xecutive order**: (in the US) an <font color="#de7802">official order from the president </font>that becomes the law
    *Yesterday the president issued an executive order to change the nation’s immigration laws.*

**<span style="font-size: larger; font-weight: bold;">A</span>spiring**: /əˈspaɪərɪŋ/ wanting to start the career or activity that is mentioned 有志于……的
     - *Aspiring musicians need hours of practice every day.*
