Name: Maxwell Laurence — Maxwell in honor of  Clerk Maxwell; Laurence for a British feel.

Ethnic Group: Han Chinese

Hometown: Huanggang, Hubei — close to Wuhan; easy weekend trips home by intercity express，just 1.5 hours.

Reasons I like it:
People can walk along the path without worrying about getting hit by e-bikes. The river is an important part of who I am. By its banks, in my hometown, I feel much closer to my true self.
Food: Local ramen — distinct from Lanzhou ramen.

Scenery: Riverside walks; fireflies in the woods in midsummer.  
*Along the Yangtze River, there is a forest park. Beyond the vast stretch of woods, the river comes into view. During the blue hour, the lights from the river islands and the massive steel equipment at the factories on the opposite bank are faintly visible. In summer, the sky is often filled with beautiful, fiery sunset clouds. As you stroll along the riverbank, you may occasionally come across abandoned houses that have stood there for decades.

Climate: as the same as Wuhan, but slightly cooler.

Tourist Attractions: Dongpo Red Cliff — tourist trap; behind my home — hills, walls, sunsets.
Personally I do not recommend anyone to visit anywhere specifically. Just enjoy a life untouched by urbanization.

Likes: Low cost of living; slow pace of life; rural areas. 

Wish: May my hometown stay forever as I remember it.
