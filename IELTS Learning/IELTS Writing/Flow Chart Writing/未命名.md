#### 1.3
- 每一个步骤都要用**不同的动词**描述
- 经常用**passive voice**

At the beginning of the process, limestone and clay are crushed by a crusher, turning into powder.
When crushing the raw materials is completed, the powder is placed in a mixer, where/in which it's blended
The mixture is conveyed through tunnel to a heater, before being rotated and heated.
When comes out from the heater, it is ground by a grinder into cement.
The final phase of the process is packaging the cement in bags for the further processing
